    Pick a recording. Labels stay hidden until you freeze and reveal.

    Start an investigation. The agent picks analyses; every tool call and output is shown.

    See the suspect. The bearing explodes and the suspected ring highlights.
  5. 5
    Freeze, then reveal. Your conclusion locks before the documented fault appears.
