## Assembly Sequence
1. Mount rear and front bearings on shaft in cylindrical housing, retain with lock nuts and end caps with oil seals
2. Attach case bracket to housing and case plate with labyrinth ring and seal ring housing
3. Fit shaft sleeve, fixed seal and impeller on shaft end, install movable seal retainer on push rods
4. Install push rod housing, springs, yoke, spider, actuator and weights for centrifugal seal actuation
5. Close volute case over impeller and support housing on base pedestal
