## Assembly Sequence
1. Seat bearing plate 11 on button surfaces 39 of cover 10 with dowels 41 in locating holes 37, weld at 52
2. Insert outer races 21 of tapered bearings 22 against annular portions 45, insert ball bearings 29 34
3. Mount bull gear 25 with snap ring 26 on quill shaft 24, intermediate gear 30 with spacer 31 on shaft 28
4. Place preassembled shafts in bearing bores 16 17, mesh bull gear to pinion 27 and gear 30 to pinion 32
5. Join housing halves via flange dowels 43 in holes 44, bolt 53 with gasket 15, fit seals 49 50 and cap 51
