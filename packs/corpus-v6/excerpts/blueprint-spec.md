- **Cylinder Bank R (Pos. 6)**: Right cylinder bank of V
- **Fuel Rail L (Pos. 7)**: Distributes fuel to left bank injectors
- **Direct Fuel Injector (Pos. 8)**: Direct-injection fuel delivery into cylinders
- **Iridium Spark Plugs (Pos. 9)**: Ignites mixture in cylinders
- **Oil Pan Sump (Pos. 10)**: Lower oil reservoir below crankshaft
