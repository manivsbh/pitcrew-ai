# Rotron fan motor (US 3,513,339) — Blueprint & Component Specification

## Confirmed Components
- **Tubular Bearing Support (Pos. 22)**: Thin-walled thermally conductive tube carrying bearing
- **Inner Sleeve (Pos. 34)**: Long sleeve tightly fitting shaft in cantilever
- **Shaft (Pos. 38)**: Rotating shaft in stationary bearing
- **Stator housing (Pos. 14)**: Base 14a plus cylindrical wall 14b housing stator
- **Sleeve Bearing (Pos. 26)**: Stationary sintered sleeve bearing
- **Cup-shaped Hub (Pos. 30)**: Rotor hub with end wall 30a and side wall 30b
- **Outer Sleeve (Pos. 36)**: Short outer sleeve engaging rotor end ring
- **Rotor Stack (Pos. 40)**: Laminated rotor stack with conductors
- **Fan Blades (Pos. 64)**: Blades on rotor hub side wall
- **Stator Stack (Pos. 16)**: Annular laminations fitted in housing recess
- **Center Section (Pos. 32)**: Shaft and rotor supporting section
- **End Ring (Pos. 42)**: Stepped end ring engaging outer sleeve
- **Rubber Plug (Pos. 53)**: Dust seal for bearing
- **Circular Opening (Pos. 20)**: Central opening in housing base
- **Annular Rim (Pos. 24)**: Axially extending rim retaining tubular member
- **Retaining Ring (Pos. 46)**: Snap ring on shaft free end
- **Sealing Ring (Pos. 52)**: Foam seal against lubricant escape
- **Rigid Ring (Pos. 54)**: Rigid ring holding rubber plug
- **Venturi Ring (Pos. 60)**: Concentric venturi around fan
- **Stator Winding (Pos. 18)**: Windings on stator stack at periphery
- **Spokes (Pos. 62)**: Radial arms supporting venturi
- **End Ring (Pos. 44)**: Opposite rotor end ring
- **Thrust Washers (Pos. 48)**: Washers between inner sleeve and bearing

## Assembly Sequence
1. Fit stator stack and windings into housing outer wall
2. Force-fit tubular support with sleeve bearing into housing rim
3. Assemble shaft into inner sleeve and rotor between sleeves
4. Insert shaft through bearing, add seals washers and snap ring
5. Attach fan blades and venturi ring to rotor/housing
