the motor to the Base (Pos. 90), then slide the motor backward on the base. If sliding provisions are not available on the base, then use

deep socket, fully unscrew the Shaft Seal from the Motor Stool (Pos. 2) or Pump Head
 (Pos. 77) on N models. 
· Sliding the Shaft Seal up and off the Shaft (Pos. 51).

 (1) Cavitation the implosion of vapor "bubbles"
