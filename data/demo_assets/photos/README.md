# Real-World Demo Photos Drop-In Folder

Drop your phone/camera workbench photos here (`JPG`, `PNG`, or `WEBP`) —
they will automatically appear as 1-click buttons in the **Blueprints -> Image-to-Assembly Guide** tab!

Recommended 4 photos to capture:
1. `real-bearing-photo.jpg` — ball bearing or motor shaft close-up
2. `real-workbench-exploded.jpg` — 3–6 parts laid out on a table for live SAM 2.1 assembly steps
3. `real-hand-sketch-blueprint.jpg` — whiteboard or paper sketch with Pos. 1..5 callouts + mm dimensions
4. `real-worn-seal.jpg` — rusted/worn bolt, seal, or bearing race to trigger automatic Repair Kit PO
